    I'm Mohammed Moinuddin, a Bachelor of Computer Applications student specialising in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at NMKRV Autonomous College.

    What pulls me in sits where AI meets everyday work: agents that take on tasks, automations that remove busywork, and tools that make research, analysis and writing faster. I'm interested in how these pieces connect into systems people can actually use.

    I learn by trying things. Alongside my degree I've been working through courses on AI agents, workflow automation, data analysis and AI in business, and I use each one as a reason to test an idea instead of only collecting another course name.

    My view of useful AI is simple. It should save someone time, make a decision clearer, or open up something that was out of reach. I'm early in the journey, and this site is where I document it as it happens.
